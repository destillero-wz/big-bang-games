🎡 How to play Wheel of Wazoku

  1. Raise your hand in Teams to take a turn. The host picks you and spins the wheel.
  2. The wheel decides what each letter is worth. Call a consonant: every time it appears you win the wheel value (e.g. 3 × 500 = 1,500).
  3. Got one? You can spin again, buy a vowel (costs 250) or solve the puzzle.
  4. Wrong letter, Lose a Turn or a wrong guess at the answer → the next raised hand goes.
  5. 💀 Bankrupt wipes the points you've won on this puzzle!
  6. Solve the puzzle for a +1,000 bonus. The most points at the end wins.

✏️ Edit puzzles

Letters, spaces and simple punctuation ( ' - & ! ? , . ) are fine. Keep each puzzle under about 50 characters so it fits the board. Saved in this browser.
