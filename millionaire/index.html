💷 Who Wants to Be a Wazillionaire?

Teams poll:

    💷 How to play

    1. We play as one team, climbing the ladder from £100 to £1,000,000.
    2. Each question goes up on screen, and the host launches a Teams poll with the same A/B/C/D options.
    3. Everyone votes. The majority answer is the team's final answer.
    4. A wrong answer costs one of the team's ❤️ 3 lives, but we carry on up the ladder.
    5. Lose all 3 lives and we leave with the last safe amount: £1,000 or £32,000 🔒.
    6. Lifelines, once each: 50:50 removes two wrong answers, 👥 Ask the Audience shows how 100 people answered, and 🔄 Switch swaps the question for a new one.

    👥 Ask the Audience

    An AI-simulated audience of 100 people: 50 from the UK and 50 from Colombia, with a mix of gamers, non-gamers, quiz fans and guessers. Not real survey data.

    ✏️ Edit questions

    15 ladder questions, plus 3 spare questions for the 🔄 Switch lifeline (easy for Q1–5, medium for Q6–10, hard for Q11–15). Choose the correct answer for each. If you change a question, its Ask the Audience results are estimated instead of using the simulated survey. Saved in this browser only.
