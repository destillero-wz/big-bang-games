    ⬜ How to play Blankety Blank

    1. Each round, one person is the ⭐ star.
    2. A fill-in-the-blank prompt appears. Everyone writes down what they think the star will say. The star writes their real answer. Keep it to yourself!
    3. On "3, 2, 1… POST!" everyone posts their answer in the Teams chat at the same time.
    4. The star's answer is revealed. Matched the star? +1 point (close matches count).
    5. React 😂 to the funniest answer in the chat. Funniest answer gets +1 bonus.
    6. Next round, someone new is the star. Most points at the end wins!
