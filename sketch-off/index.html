🎃 Spooky Sketch-Off

  1. Grab some paper and a pen. Any pens, pencils or highlighters will do!
  2. A Halloween prompt appears. Draw your own take on it before the timer runs out.
  3. When it's pens down, take a photo of your drawing and post it in the Teams chat.
  4. React ❤️ to your favourite. The most hearts wins the round! (Warm-ups are just for fun.)

No artistic talent required. The more creative (or questionable) the better 👻
