    🔍 How to play

    1. Watch the original scene for 30 seconds.
    2. Then watch it again. This time there are 7 differences. Some are there all the time, and some only happen for a moment.
    3. As soon as you spot a difference, type it in the Teams chat. Don't wait for the end!
    4. The host reveals each difference. Everyone who spotted it scores: Easy 1, Medium 2, Hard 3. The first person to call it gets a ⚡ +1 bonus.
    5. Play both scenes. The leaderboard adds up your total.
    6. Use ⇄ Compare to flick between the original and the changed scene, and the slider to scrub through.
