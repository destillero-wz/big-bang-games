## Spooky Pictionary

Each artist opens the link on their own laptop:
1. Click **🎃 Get my secret word**. The word opens in a small pop-up window. Use **🎲 New word** in the pop-up to choose easy, medium or hard.
2. In Teams, share **this browser window**, not the whole screen, so the pop-up with the word stays private.
3. Click **▶ Start timer** (45, 60 or 90 seconds) and draw. You can choose from 8 colours and 4 brush sizes, and use the eraser, undo (Ctrl+Z) and clear.
4. When someone guesses it, click **✅ Got it!**. When the timer runs out, the word is revealed automatically. Then click **🗑 Clear** and hand over to the next artist.

If the browser blocks the pop-up, the button changes to **👀 Hold to peek**. Hold it down to see the word, and do this before you start sharing.

## Wazoku Says (Family Feud)

- **Setup:** add questions in **✏️ Edit questions**. Each has up to 8 answers, with points usually meaning how many people out of 100 said it. You can also load a ready-made set: Halloween, autumn & work life; Space & aliens; or Classic Family Feud style. Add players in **🏆 Scores**.
- **Playing:** read the question. When someone names an answer that's on the board, click it (or press **1–8**) to flip it, then click their name, or type a new person's name, to give them its points. Their name appears on the answer. Click a revealed answer again to move the points to someone else or take them back. For a wrong guess, click **❌ Wrong answer** (or press **X**) to show the big X with a buzzer sound. Use **← →** to move between questions.
- **↺ Reset game** hides all the answers again and keeps the scores.

## Jeopardy

- **Playing:** click a tile to show the question. Press **Space** to reveal the answer and **Esc** to go back to the board. Used tiles go dark.
- **Daily Double:** a splash screen appears first, and the question is worth double.
- **Scores (optional):** in **🏆 Scores**, add players. After revealing an answer, click the name of whoever got it to give them the points. You can also adjust scores with the +100 and −100 buttons.
- **Editing:** use **✏️ Edit questions** to change the title, categories, questions and answers, and to pick the Daily Double (or use **🎲 Random**). Edits are saved in that browser only, so use **Download questions file** to keep a copy and **Load questions file** to use it on another computer.
- **↺ Reset board** makes every tile available again and keeps the scores.
- **Pictures:** any question can show a picture. In the editor, paste a picture link into "Picture link (optional)". Country silhouettes are in `jeopardy/img/`, for example `img/italy.svg`. They're drawn from Natural Earth map data, which is public domain.
- **Ready-made sets:** in the editor, choose a set and click **Load ready-made set**. The sets are "2026 in Review + Name That Country" (the default) and "General knowledge".

Each game lives in its own folder. Add a card for each new game to `index.html`.

## Spooky Sketch-Off

Share this screen on Teams. Each prompt shows big on screen with its level and a countdown. Press **▶ Start drawing** (or **Space**) to start it, then use **+30s**, **Pause** and **← →** as you need. When time's up, a **Pens down** screen tells everyone to post a photo of their drawing in the chat and react ❤️ to their favourite. Warm-ups have no vote. After the last prompt there's a final "best of the session" vote. **✏️ Edit prompts** lets you change the prompts, levels and times.
