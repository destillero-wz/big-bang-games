🎃 Spooky Pictionary

  1. Click 🎃 Get my secret word. It opens in a small pop-up window that only you can see.
  2. In Teams, click Share and choose this browser window, not your whole screen, so the pop-up stays secret.
  3. Click ▶ Start timer and draw! No letters, numbers or words allowed.
  4. When someone guesses it, click ✅ Got it! If time runs out, the word is revealed.
  5. Click 🗑 Clear and hand over to the next artist.
